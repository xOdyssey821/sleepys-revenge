$leepy's Revenge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how $leepy's Revenge ("the Bot") handles information when used on Discord servers.

2. Information We Handle

The Bot may process or store:

  • Discord user IDs and server IDs.
  • Username and member information needed for bot features.
  • XP, levels, and virtual economy balances.
  • Server configuration, custom commands, and custom links.
  • Moderation, ticket, and giveaway information.
  • Messages needed for commands and moderation features.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the Bot's features, including moderation, levels, economy, support tickets, and server configuration.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Data may be stored in the Bot's database. Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate the relevant features or meet applicable obligations.

5. Data Removal

Server administrators may request deletion of server-related data. Contact the Bot owner using the support method provided in the Bot's Discord server.

6. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord. Music features may use third-party services to obtain audio information. Their own policies may apply.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or data removal requests, contact the Bot owner through the official $leepy's Revenge support server.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
